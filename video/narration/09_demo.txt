Here is the tool. A fresh key is generated and split. It is never printed, and never written to disk. Any three custodians can use it: it goes straight into another program, through a pipe. Now custodian one changes a single byte of their share. CAIRN refuses, and outputs nothing. With a fourth share, it recovers the key, and names the cheater. And a share stolen before a refresh no longer combines with the new ones.
