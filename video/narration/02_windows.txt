Shamir's secret sharing is perfect on paper. On a real computer, a shared key still leaks through five windows. Share files get stolen. A patient attacker collects shares over months. Memory gets dumped. Arithmetic leaks through timing. And one dishonest custodian can lie, and walk away with the key. CAIRN closes each window with linear algebra, and with no computational assumption at all. No AES. No hash. No hard problem.
