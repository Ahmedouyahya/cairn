Shares must also change over time. To refresh L and R without ever computing their inner product, I use skew-symmetric matrices: A times R is always orthogonal to R. My first design used two steps, and it passed every correctness test. But the new L was still tied to the old R. Read R before a refresh and L after it, and the value falls out exactly. My lab measured a distance of one: a total break. A third step fixes it. The leakage drops to exactly p to the minus, ell minus one, as the proof predicts, to every digit.
